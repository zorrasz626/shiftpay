# ShiftPay privacy policy

**Last updated: 1 November 2026**

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the ShiftPay Android app. The short version: **your rota, pay, payslip and tax details stay on your phone.** ShiftPay has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no server of its own.

## Who we are

ShiftPay is made by Luis Santos, an independent developer based in the UK ("we", "us"). For the small amount of personal data described below, we are the data controller under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Contact: **zorrasz@protonmail.com**

## What stays on your phone

Everything you type into ShiftPay is stored only in a database inside the app on your device. This includes:

- your jobs, pay rates, premium and overtime rules, and pay period settings;
- your rota patterns, shift templates and shifts;
- your holiday entitlement and holiday taken;
- payslip figures you enter, and the results of payslip checks;
- tax settings for the net-pay estimate (tax code, student loan plan, workplace pension);
- app settings, such as reminder times.

We never receive this information. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, unless you choose to share a file yourself (see "Backups and exports" below).

**Android backup.** If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may copy the app's data to your Google account, as it does for other apps. That backup is run by Google under your Google account settings and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We cannot see it.

## Purchases (ShiftPay Pro)

ShiftPay Pro is sold through Google Play. We use **RevenueCat** to manage purchases and check whether Pro is active on your device.

- **Google Play** handles the payment. We never see your card or bank details. Google processes your payment under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **RevenueCat** receives a random, anonymous ID that it creates for your copy of the app, plus your purchase and subscription details from Google Play (which product, purchase dates, price, currency, renewal and trial status). Like any internet request, this also includes technical details such as your IP address, the app version and your Android version. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email address, or any of your pay, rota or tax details.

We use this information only to unlock Pro, restore purchases and manage subscriptions. Our lawful basis is that it is needed to provide what you bought (contract). RevenueCat acts as our processor; see [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy). Purchase records are kept for as long as needed to manage your purchases and meet legal and accounting duties.

ShiftPay talks to RevenueCat when the app starts or returns to the foreground, when you open the upgrade screen, and when you buy or restore a purchase.

## UK bank holiday dates

To keep bank holiday dates up to date, the app downloads the public list from GOV.UK (`https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays.json`) at most once a week. Nothing about you is sent with this request, but, as with any website, GOV.UK's servers see your IP address. See the [GOV.UK privacy notice](https://www.gov.uk/help/privacy-notice). If the download fails, the app uses the dates it already has.

## Shift reminders

Shift reminders are scheduled on your phone by the app itself. They do not use a push notification service, and no notification token or reminder content leaves your device. Android will ask for your permission before the app can show notifications; you can turn this off at any time in Android settings.

## Backups and exports you share yourself

With ShiftPay Pro you can export a backup file (JSON) and payslip check reports (PDF or CSV). The app creates the file on your phone and opens Android's share sheet. **You decide where it goes** (for example, your own cloud storage, email or a message to your payroll team). Once you share a file, the app or service you send it to handles it under its own privacy policy. These files contain the details you entered, including pay and tax settings, so keep them somewhere safe.

When you import a backup, the app reads only the file you pick, on your device.

## What we do not do

- No accounts or sign-in.
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting tools.
- No selling or sharing of your data with advertisers or data brokers.
- No location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

## Deleting your data

- To delete everything stored by the app, **uninstall ShiftPay**, or go to Android **Settings > Apps > ShiftPay > Storage > Clear storage**.
- Backup and export files you shared are not deleted by the app; delete them wherever you saved them.
- To cancel a subscription, use the Google Play Store (**Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions**). Uninstalling does not cancel it.
- To ask for your purchase record at RevenueCat to be deleted, email us at zorrasz@protonmail.com. Because we do not know who you are, we may ask for your Google Play order number to find it.

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to data portability. Most of your data never reaches us, so you control it directly on your phone. For anything held by RevenueCat on our behalf, contact us at zorrasz@protonmail.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office ([ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk)).

## Children

ShiftPay is meant for working adults. It is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Pay estimates

ShiftPay's figures are estimates only — not financial or tax advice. Always check with your employer/payroll.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. If a change affects what data leaves your phone, we will also mention it in the app's release notes.
